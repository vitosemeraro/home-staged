360° virtual staging: which tools support panoramas and tours

What 360° virtual staging is, which AI staging tools in our 2026 comparison support panoramas and tours, how to check a staged tour and what to do if your tool works on flat photos only.

Updated 26 November 2026 · staged.ai editorial team · 5 min read

Quick answer

360° virtual staging adds furniture to a full panoramic photo, so the room stays furnished as the viewer turns around in a virtual tour. Of the seven tools in our October 2026 comparison, Pedra (360° tours) and Collov AI (360° panoramas on higher tiers) cover it. staged.ai does not: it stages standard listing photos, which you can publish next to an unstaged tour.

A normal listing photo shows one corner of a room; a 360° photo shows all of it. That makes staging harder: the furniture has to look right from every angle the viewer can turn to, across a wide panoramic image, without bending walls or windows. This guide explains what 360° virtual staging is, which tools in our virtual staging software comparison support it, and what to do if your tool of choice works on flat photos only. staged.ai is our own product; we say clearly where it does not fit.

What is 360° virtual staging?

360° virtual staging is virtual staging applied to a panoramic photo instead of a standard one. A 360° camera captures the whole room in one shot, usually saved as an equirectangular image twice as wide as it is tall, which a tour viewer wraps into a sphere you can drag around. Staging that image means placing furniture that stays consistent as the view rotates, so the room still reads as one real space from every direction.

It is different from three things it is often confused with:

  • Standard virtual staging: one flat photo, one point of view. This is what most AI staging tools do, staged.ai included.
  • Multi-angle staging: several flat photos of the same room staged with matching furniture. Collov AI lists it as a separate feature on its Advanced plan.
  • 3D tours: a navigable model of the whole home built from many scans, where you move from room to room rather than only turning on the spot.

Is 360° virtual staging worth it for your listings?

It depends on whether your listings already use 360° tours. If you shoot panoramas for every property, an empty tour next to staged photos can feel inconsistent, and a tool that stages the panorama keeps the two aligned. If you publish standard photos only, 360° staging adds a new shoot, a new tool and a new cost for the same listing.

How do you check a 360° staged tour before publishing?

  1. Turn a full circle: look in every direction, including the ceiling and the floor near the camera, not just the main view.
  2. Look at the seam: the left and right edges of the panorama meet behind the viewer; furniture crossing that line should join cleanly.
  3. Check straight lines: door frames, windows and wall corners must stay straight and in the same place as in the original.
  4. Check scale: a sofa that is too small makes the room look bigger than it is, and in a 360° view the mistake is visible from more angles.
  5. Label it: mark the tour as virtually staged and keep the unstaged version available, as explained in our virtual staging disclosure rules.

What if your staging tool does not support 360°?

You can still use it for the part of the listing most buyers see first: the photo gallery. Stage the standard photos of the key rooms, label them, and keep the 360° tour unstaged as the honest record of the empty home. Buyers get a furnished picture of the space in the gallery and the real proportions in the tour. Frequently asked questions

Can you virtually stage a 360° photo?

Yes, with a tool that supports panoramas. In our October 2026 comparison, Pedra includes 360° virtual tours and Collov AI offers 360° panoramas and 3D tours on its higher tiers. Most AI staging tools, staged.ai included, work on standard flat photos only.

Does staged.ai support 360° photos or virtual tours?

No. staged.ai stages standard listing photos, one room at a time, and does not make 360° tours, video or floor plans. You can use it for the photo gallery and keep your 360° tour unstaged, or use Pedra or Collov AI if staged panoramas are essential.

How much does 360° virtual staging cost?

It depends on the tool and plan. Collov AI's pricing page lists 20 credits for a 360° panorama or a 3D tour, on plans billed annually. Pedra lists 360° tours as unlimited on its Pro plan at €29 a month. Check the official sites, because plans and credit costs change often.

Do I have to disclose a virtually staged 360° tour?

Yes, the same rules apply as for staged photos. From 2 August 2026 the EU AI Act requires disclosure of AI-generated or manipulated images that could pass as real, and in the UK and US misleading property images breach advertising and consumer rules. Label the tour and keep the unstaged version available.
