Virtual renovation: see the finished room before any work starts

AI virtual renovation: see a room with new floors, walls and finishes from a single photo, with works costs estimated for Italian cities. Try staged.ai free.

Quick answer

Virtual renovation uses AI to show how a room will look after the works, with new floors, walls and finishes, starting from a real photo. With staged.ai you choose the Renovation goal, set budget, style, city and a Sikkens colour palette, and get the renovated image plus a list of works with full estimated costs.

Virtual renovation lets you see a room after the works from nothing more than a photo: new floors, fresh walls, updated finishes, with the real structure left exactly as it is. On staged.ai (home-staged.ai) this is the Renovation goal, which pairs the after image with a list of works and full costs estimated for the city. It is built for people selling a property that needs work, people buying one, and anyone weighing up whether a renovation is worth it, especially in Italy, where the cost data comes from.

What is virtual renovation?

It is a photorealistic simulation of renovation work, generated by AI from a photo of the property. Unlike virtual staging, which adds furniture and decor, virtual renovation changes what would normally need builders: floors, walls and finishes.

Its value is in making a possibility concrete. A dated flat in a listing photo just looks "in need of work"; the same flat with the works simulated becomes a project a buyer can assess with numbers in hand.

With staged.ai, the steps are:

What can you simulate?

You can simulate the finishing works that change how a room looks, without altering its geometry:

  • Floors: new flooring, different materials and colours.
  • Walls: paint and finishes, with colours drawn from the Sikkens palette.
  • Finishes: the surfaces that make a room feel new or dated.

Walls, doors and windows always stay where they are, thanks to the geometric lock in the prompts, with dedicated rules for kitchens and bathrooms. This is not a tool for planning demolition or moving partition walls: that needs a surveyor or architect and a floor plan. If you want to see the room cleared before renovating, create a variant with Empty Room.

Virtual renovation or virtual staging: which do you need?

It depends on what is holding buyers back. If the room is sound but bare, furniture is enough; if the floors and walls say "old house", you need to show the works.

How much does renovation cost in Italy in 2026?

A full renovation in Italy typically costs from €1,500 to over €2,000 per square metre, but it depends heavily on the scope and the city. Reference figures for 2026 from >Il Giornale Edile, in euros:

Light renovation €700–1,000/m²
Medium renovation €1,000–1,500/m²
Full renovation €1,500–2,000+/m²
Painting €12–25/m²
Flooring and finishes €60–150/m²
Complete bathroom €6,000–12,000

Who is virtual renovation for?

It is for anyone deciding on a home that is not yet what it could be:

  • Real estate agents (estate agents, in UK terms) with properties that need work: show the potential in the listing alongside a ballpark cost. See AI virtual staging for real estate agents.
  • Buyers, including international buyers looking at Italian homes: understand what you are buying and what you might spend after completion.
  • Owners choosing between selling as-is and renovating first.
  • Interior designers presenting several options as v1 and v2 of a project.

How do you publish a virtual renovation without misleading anyone?

Always present it as a simulation, with the original photo alongside. A renovated photo shows a home that does not exist yet, and viewers need to know that. In the EU, Article 50 of the AI Act on AI-generated or manipulated images applies from 2 August 2026, and the press already calls misleading property photos "housefishing" (>Euronews). UK guidance warns against altering structural features or defects (>Intellopro). A caption such as "AI renovation concept" is the simplest fix. Read more in our guide to virtual staging disclosure rules.

What staged.ai's virtual renovation does not do

It does not produce floor plans, 3D renders, videos or 360° tours; tools such as Pedra or Collov offer those. It does not replace a surveyor, a site visit or contractor quotes, which will account for systems, structure and permits. Cost estimates are calibrated on the Italian market. Frequently asked questions

Is there an app to see a house renovated from a photo?

Yes. AI virtual renovation tools start from a real photo and show the room with new floors, walls and finishes. staged.ai does this with its Renovation goal, keeping the structure, doors and windows unchanged, and adds a list of works with costs estimated for the city. Cost estimates are calibrated on the Italian market.

How much does it cost to renovate a home in Italy in 2026?

According to Il Giornale Edile, a light renovation in Italy costs around €700–1,000 per square metre in 2026, a medium one €1,000–1,500 and a full one €1,500–2,000 or more. Location matters: a full renovation runs €1,100–1,600 per square metre in Milan and €750–1,150 in southern cities such as Naples and Bari.

Can I use staged.ai's estimates as a contractor quote?

No. They are indicative estimates calibrated on the city you set, based on Italian market sources such as OMI, Idealista, FIAIP and EAHSP. Use them to understand the order of magnitude and compare options. Real decisions need a site visit and written quotes from contractors, who will assess wiring, plumbing and the property's condition.

Can I use a virtual renovation in a property listing?

Yes, as long as it is clearly presented as a simulation. Publish it with a caption such as 'AI renovation concept' next to the original photo. In the EU, AI Act transparency rules apply from 2 August 2026; the UK prohibits misleading property marketing; and US MLS rules generally require edited photos to be disclosed.

Does it work for properties outside Italy?

The visual simulation works on any room, anywhere. The cost estimates do not travel as well: they reflect Italian prices and sources. If your property is outside Italy, use the renovated images for inspiration and planning, and rely on local contractors for the budget.
