staged.ai vs Virtual Staging AI: an honest comparison

Looking for a Virtual Staging AI alternative? A fair comparison with staged.ai on features, pricing and use cases, and when each one fits best. October 2026.

Updated 4 October 2026 · staged.ai editorial team · 5 min read

Quick answer

Virtual Staging AI, acquired by Zillow in 2024, is a straightforward tool for staging and emptying photos, priced from $16 to $79 a month by photo volume and geared to the US market. staged.ai is an alternative built in Europe: €19 a month, four edit types, room-by-room analysis and cost estimates calibrated on Italian prices.

If you use Virtual Staging AI and are weighing up alternatives, the key difference is what you get beyond the picture. Virtual Staging AI does one job well: staging or emptying photos quickly, with simple plans based on how many images you process. staged.ai (home-staged.ai) pairs staging with renovation previews, a room-by-room analysis and estimated costs for the works, bundled into a quote you can share with clients. This comparison, updated October 2026, covers features, pricing and when each tool makes more sense.

Who is Virtual Staging AI best for?

Virtual Staging AI is best for real estate agents and photographers, especially in the US, who want to stage or empty photos with a simple workflow and predictable per-photo pricing. Since October 2024 it has been part of Zillow, America's largest property portal, and its positioning reflects that market: dollar pricing, plus content and styling aimed at American buyers.

Its strengths are easy to see: plans starting at just a handful of photos a month, unlimited regenerations on uploaded photos, staging plus furniture removal, and an Enterprise tier aimed explicitly at teams, brokerages and photographers. If you list US homes and only need the image, it's a sensible choice.

When should you choose Virtual Staging AI?

Virtual Staging AI makes sense when you work in the US market or only need the staged image, without analysis. In particular if you:

  • list properties in the United States and want styles and workflows designed for that market;
  • work within the Zillow ecosystem and prefer a supplier that's part of it;
  • need a very small plan for a few photos a month (6 on Basic);
  • are a photographer or brokerage with high volumes and want predictable per-photo pricing;
  • don't need quotes or cost estimates, just furnished or emptied photos.

When should you choose staged.ai?

staged.ai makes sense when you want the image to double as a sales tool, especially for property in Italy or Europe. In particular if you:

  • want to stage, empty, renovate or retouch in one place, choosing the purpose photo by photo;
  • sell to overseas buyers looking at Italian homes and want a virtual renovation with itemised costs for that city;
  • want a branded, shareable quote with your logo and colours, read-only for clients, which also suits photographers reselling a white label virtual staging service;
  • want images to stay true to the real room, thanks to a geometry lock that preserves walls, doors, windows, floors, kitchens and fixed bathroom fittings;
  • prefer euro pricing with a free trial.

The honest limits: staged.ai doesn't do video, 360° tours or floor plans; its cost estimates are Italian, so outside Italy treat them as indicative; and any estimate should be checked with a site visit and real contractor quotes.

How do you switch from Virtual Staging AI to staged.ai?

Switching takes minutes because both tools start from photos:

  1. Start the free trial: 10 credits, all four purposes.
  2. Upload original photos, not ones that are already staged.
  3. Pick a purpose for each photo: for furnished rooms, try Empty Room first and then a Home Staging variant. See how to remove furniture from photos with AI.
  4. Set budget, style, city and palette, then compare the output with what you get today.
  5. Share the link with a seller to test how they react to the quote.

Whichever tool you use, label staged images. In the US, the NAR Code of Ethics requires a "true picture" in advertising and most MLSs expect disclosure; in the EU, Article 50 of the AI Act applies from 2 August 2026, amid growing press attention to "housefishing" (>Euronews). See our guide to virtual staging disclosure rules.

What about agents in the UK, Ireland and the rest of Europe?

Both tools work on any property photo, so either can serve European agents. The practical differences are currency, rules and extras. Virtual Staging AI bills in dollars and is designed around US listings. staged.ai bills in euros, offers an English interface and adds analysis and costs, although those cost figures are calibrated on Italian cities. For UK estate agents, the bigger issue is compliance: consumer protection law bans misleading property images, so whichever tool you choose, keep originals, never alter structural features and label every staged photo clearly.

Why does staging matter, whichever tool you choose?

Because it helps buyers picture themselves in the home: according to the >NAR 2025 Profile of Home Staging, 83% of buyers' agents agree, and 29% of sellers' agents saw offers 1–10% higher. Frequently asked questions

What is Virtual Staging AI?

Virtual Staging AI (virtualstagingai.app) is an AI virtual staging service that furnishes empty rooms and removes furniture from photos. It was acquired by Zillow in October 2024 and is strongly oriented to the US market. Plans are based on the number of photos uploaded per month, with unlimited regenerations on the photos you upload.

How much does Virtual Staging AI cost?

As of October 2026, plans are Basic at $16 a month for 6 photos, Standard at $19 for 20 photos, Professional at $39 for 60 photos and Enterprise at $79 for 150 photos, with annual billing available. Always check the official pricing page before subscribing, as plans and allowances can change.

Why would I choose staged.ai over Virtual Staging AI?

Mainly if you need more than the image. staged.ai adds renovation previews, a room-by-room analysis and itemised cost estimates with a shareable, white-label quote. Those estimates are calibrated on Italian market data, so it fits best for properties in Italy, overseas buyers renovating there, and European agents and photographers who want euro pricing.

Is Virtual Staging AI good for agents outside the US?

It can be, since staging an image works anywhere, but the product is clearly designed around the US market, with dollar pricing and Zillow ownership. Agents in the UK, Ireland or the EU should check how styles and support suit their market. staged.ai offers an English interface but was built for the Italian market first.

Can I try staged.ai before cancelling Virtual Staging AI?

Yes. The staged.ai trial is free and includes 10 credits with all four purposes: Home Staging, Renovation, Retouch & Declutter and Empty Room. Upload the original photos of a couple of listings, compare the results with your current output, and decide only after you've also seen the analysis and the quote.
