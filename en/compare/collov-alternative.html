staged.ai vs Collov AI: features, pricing and fit

Looking for a Collov AI alternative? Compare staged.ai and Collov on features, pricing, credits and use cases, including when Collov is the better choice.

Updated 4 October 2026 · staged.ai editorial team · 5 min read

Quick answer

Collov AI is a feature-rich platform covering staging, furniture removal, advanced photo editing and, on higher tiers, video and 360°/3D tours, from $19 a month billed annually. staged.ai is a more focused alternative at €19 a month with no annual commitment, built for faithful staging plus renovation cost estimates calibrated on Italian prices.

If you're looking for a Collov AI alternative, first decide whether you need a very broad visual toolbox or a tool that turns a photo into a proposal with numbers attached. Collov AI is feature-rich, with staging, furniture removal, advanced editing and, on higher plans, video and 360°/3D tours. staged.ai (home-staged.ai) offers fewer visual effects, but pairs staging with a room-by-room analysis, estimated costs for the works and a shareable quote, while keeping the image faithful to the real room. This comparison, updated October 2026, covers features, pricing and when each tool fits best.

Who is Collov AI best for?

Collov AI is best for agents, photographers and teams who want many visual editing tools in one platform and are happy with an annual subscription. On top of staging and furniture removal with unlimited regenerations, the Advanced plan adds multi-angle staging, chat-based editing, material overlays, twilight conversion, seasonal changes and weather removal. Premium adds video tours, 360° panoramas and 3D tours, and Enterprise includes API access.

One point worth highlighting in its favour: Collov lets you add a virtual staging disclaimer in a single click, which makes transparent marketing easier.

When should you choose Collov AI?

Collov makes sense when you want a wide range of visual effects and immersive content. In particular if you:

  • want advanced edits such as twilight shots, seasonal changes, material swaps or pool and lawn effects;
  • need video tours, 360° panoramas or 3D tours generated from photos;
  • want multi-angle staging that stays consistent across several shots of the same room;
  • work at high volume and need an API for integration;
  • prefer a large annual credit allowance and are happy to pay a year up front;
  • want a one-click virtual staging disclaimer.

When should you choose staged.ai?

staged.ai makes sense when you want images that help you win instructions or sell by talking about real works and costs. In particular if you:

  • deal with Italian properties that need work, for local or overseas buyers, and want a virtual renovation with itemised costs for that city;
  • want a renovation cost estimate from photos ready to share under your agency's logo and colours;
  • want images to stay true to the real room: the geometry lock preserves walls, doors, windows, floors, kitchens and fixed bathroom fittings, with dedicated rules for kitchens and bathrooms;
  • manage short-term rentals and want the STR analysis with potential, nightly rates and restyling ROI;
  • prefer a €19 monthly plan with no annual commitment, where room variants don't use credits.

For context, industry benchmarks put subscription AI staging at roughly $0.25–3 per photo, against $16–39 per image for staging done by hand by a designer (>aitoolsbakery, 2026), so both tools are far cheaper than the traditional route. The honest limits of staged.ai: no video, tours or floor plans; cost estimates are Italian and should be treated as indicative elsewhere; and any estimate needs a site visit and real contractor quotes.

How do the credit systems differ?

The two tools measure usage differently, so the monthly price alone won't tell you which is cheaper for you. On Collov, credits relate to the photos you process and the features you use, from a monthly allowance paid for the whole year. On staged.ai, 1 credit equals one project analysis, which can include up to 10 photos, and variants of the same room (say, a staged version and an emptied version) don't use credits at all. For a fair comparison, work out what you'd spend per listed property at your real monthly volume, not per individual photo.

How do you switch from Collov AI to staged.ai?

There's nothing to migrate, since both tools work from photos. If you're on an annual Collov plan, you can trial staged.ai alongside it until your renewal date.

  1. Start the free trial: 10 credits, all four purposes.
  2. Use the original photos of a property you've already processed in Collov, for a direct comparison.
  3. Pick a purpose for each photo and set budget, style, city and palette.
  4. Compare the full package: not just the image, but the analysis, works list and quote.
  5. Always label images: with staged.ai you add the disclosure to your listing yourself. See examples in our guide to virtual staging disclosure rules.

Frequently asked questions

What are the main differences between staged.ai and Collov AI?

Collov AI offers a wide range of visual tools: staging, furniture removal, material swaps, twilight and seasonal edits, plus video and 360° and 3D tours on higher plans. staged.ai focuses on four purposes per photo and adds a room-by-room analysis, works with estimated costs calibrated on Italian prices, and a shareable quote under your agency's branding.

How much does Collov AI cost?

Billed annually, as of October 2026 plans are Standard at $19 a month with 60 credits, Advanced at $49 with 150, Premium at $79 with 263 and Enterprise at $127 with 526 credits. You pay for the full year up front. Check the official site for current prices and any promotions running.

Does Collov AI add a virtual staging disclaimer?

Yes. Collov lets you add a virtual staging disclaimer in one click, which is genuinely useful now that Article 50 of the EU AI Act applies from 2 August 2026 and UK and US rules prohibit misleading property images. Whatever tool you use, label staged images clearly and publish the original photo alongside.

Does staged.ai do virtual tours or video like Collov?

No. staged.ai isn't 3D rendering, video or 360° tour software. It works on still photos, with staging, emptying, renovation previews and retouching. If tours and video are central to how you market property, Collov's higher tiers or a dedicated tour platform will suit you better. You can also use the two side by side.

How can I try staged.ai if I already use Collov?

Start the free staged.ai trial, which includes 10 credits and all four purposes with no annual subscription. Upload the original photos of a property you've already processed in Collov, compare the results, and look at the analysis and quote too, since that is where the two tools differ most.
