staged.ai vs Pedra: which fits your listings?

Looking for a Pedra alternative? A fair staged.ai vs Pedra comparison of features, pricing and use cases, including when Pedra is the better fit. Oct 2026.

Updated 4 October 2026 · staged.ai editorial team · 5 min read

Quick answer

Pedra is a broad real estate marketing platform covering virtual staging, video, 360° tours, floor plans and social publishing from €29 a month. staged.ai is a more focused alternative from €19 a month, built for staging that stays faithful to the real room plus itemised renovation cost estimates and a shareable quote, calibrated on Italian prices.

If you're looking for a Pedra alternative, start with one question: do you need an all-in-one content suite for your listings, or a focused tool for faithful staging and renovation cost estimates? Pedra is a broad, well-built platform covering staging, video, virtual tours and floor plans. staged.ai (home-staged.ai) does fewer things, with a narrower goal: showing the real room furnished, emptied or renovated without altering its geometry, alongside the works needed and what they're likely to cost. This comparison, updated October 2026, covers features, pricing and when each tool is the better choice.

Who is Pedra best for?

Pedra is best for agencies and property professionals who want to produce every visual asset for a listing from one platform. Beyond virtual staging and photo enhancement, it offers property videos, 360° virtual tours, 2D floor plans, floor plan renders, animated photos, social media publishing and an API for connecting to other software. The annual plan also includes a mobile app.

If your bottleneck is the volume and variety of content, it's a strong choice: teams listing many properties a month who want video and tours without booking a photographer will find Pedra very complete. It also publishes plenty of multilingual content, which helps teams working across European markets.

When should you choose Pedra?

Choose Pedra if you need more content formats than still images. Specifically, if you:

  • want to create videos and 360° virtual tours from photos without an outside supplier;
  • need floor plans and floor plan renders for your listings;
  • want to publish to social media from the same platform;
  • need an API to connect staging to your CRM or listing software;
  • prefer an annual plan with a large pool of credits to use over the year.

In these cases staged.ai isn't a replacement: it isn't 3D rendering, video or 360° tour software.

When should you choose staged.ai?

Choose staged.ai if your main goal is to show a property's potential credibly and talk numbers with your client. Specifically, if you:

  • want staged images to stay true to the real room: the geometry lock preserves walls, doors, windows, floors, kitchens and fixed bathroom fittings, with dedicated rules for kitchens and bathrooms;
  • handle properties in Italy that need work, and want a virtual renovation with an itemised estimate for that city, which is especially useful for overseas buyers;
  • need to empty rooms, including built-in kitchens and wardrobes, with a clearance estimate; see how to remove furniture from photos with AI;
  • want a shareable quote branded with your agency's logo and colours, read-only for the client;
  • manage short-term rentals and want the STR analysis with potential, nightly rates and restyling ROI;
  • want a lighter subscription: €19 a month, with room variants that don't use credits.

One honest limit: cost estimates are calibrated on the Italian market. Outside Italy, use staged.ai for the visuals and get local quotes for the numbers. Anywhere, estimates should be checked with a site visit and real contractor quotes.

How do you switch from Pedra to staged.ai?

Switching is straightforward because there's nothing to migrate: you work from photos, not 3D projects.

  1. Start with the free trial: 10 credits, all four purposes (Home Staging, Renovation, Retouch & Declutter, Empty Room).
  2. Use original photos, not ones you've already edited: staging should start from the real shot.
  3. Test a live listing and compare the result with what you publish today, paying particular attention to windows, floors and kitchens.
  4. Add your logo and colours for branded shared links.
  5. Consider using both: if you mainly use Pedra for video and tours, keep it for that and use staged.ai for staging and quotes.

Always label edited images as AI-generated; see our guide to virtual staging disclosure rules.

How do the credit systems compare?

Credits are counted differently, so compare cost per property rather than headline prices. On Pedra, staging and photo enhancement use credits, while videos, 360° tours and 2D floor plans are unlimited on the Pro plan, and floor plan renders cost 2 credits. On staged.ai, 1 credit equals one project analysis of up to 10 photos, and extra variants of the same room are free. If your listings are photo-heavy and need video too, Pedra's bundle may work out better value; if you mainly need staged images plus a costed quote, staged.ai's model is usually simpler.

Are there other Pedra alternatives?

Yes. If neither tool fits, there are plenty of others with different strengths, from US-focused staging apps to platforms with 3D tours. Industry benchmarks put subscription AI staging at roughly $0.25–3 per photo (>aitoolsbakery, 2026), so price differences are often smaller than feature differences. Frequently asked questions

What is the main difference between staged.ai and Pedra?

Pedra goes broad: alongside virtual staging it offers property videos, 360° virtual tours, floor plans, renders and social publishing. staged.ai goes deep on one workflow: staged photos that keep the real room's geometry, a room-by-room analysis and works with estimated costs for the property's city, bundled into a quote you can share under your own branding.

Is staged.ai cheaper than Pedra?

On monthly plans, yes: staged.ai Pro is €19 a month, while Pedra Pro is €29 a month with 100 credits (prices verified October 2026). Pedra also has an annual plan at €239 a year with 1,200 credits and includes tools staged.ai doesn't offer. The fairer comparison depends on how many properties you handle and what content you need.

Do staged.ai's cost estimates work outside Italy?

Only as a rough guide. staged.ai calibrates renovation and clearance costs on Italian market sources such as OMI, Idealista and FIAIP, so the figures are most reliable for homes in Italy. The visual staging, emptying and retouching work on any property anywhere, so international users mainly use it for images and Italian buyers for both.

Can I use staged.ai and Pedra together?

Yes, and for some agencies it makes sense. You could use staged.ai for faithful staging, emptying rooms and the cost-backed quote you show sellers or buyers, and Pedra for video, 360° tours and floor plans. Before doing that, check that your listing volume justifies paying for two separate subscriptions.

How do I switch from Pedra to staged.ai?

Start with the free trial, which includes 10 credits and all four purposes. Upload the original photos of a few live listings, not images you've already edited, and compare the results with your current workflow. If it works for you, set up your agency logo and colours for shared links and move to the Pro plan.
