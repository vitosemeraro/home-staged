Virtual staging software in 2026: 7 tools side by side

Seven virtual staging tools compared for 2026: features, verified pricing and who each one suits best, with a transparent methodology and no paid rankings.

Updated 13 November 2026 · staged.ai editorial team · 8 min read

Quick answer

There's no single best virtual staging software; it depends on what you need. Pedra and Collov AI also cover video and 360° tours, Virtual Staging AI is simple and US-focused, Apply Design adds hands-on editing, Homestyler is for 3D design, and staged.ai pairs faithful staging with renovation cost estimates for Italy.

There's no virtual staging software that's best for everyone: the right choice depends on what you need to produce, for which market and on what budget. If you want video and 360° tours, you need a different platform from someone staging three photos a month, or from an agent who wants to show a seller what a renovation would cost. This guide, updated October 2026, compares seven tools with a "best for" table, prices checked on official sites and a transparent methodology. One of them is staged.ai (home-staged.ai), our own product: we're saying so up front, and we treat it the same way as the others.

How did we choose and compare these tools?

We picked AI virtual staging tools that real estate professionals frequently mention and that publish information about their features and pricing, then compared them on the same criteria.

Methodology:

  1. Sources: official product and pricing pages, checked in October 2026. Where a detail wasn't public or verifiable, we say "check the official site".
  2. Criteria: types of edit (staging, emptying, renovation, retouching), extra formats (video, tours, floor plans), pricing model, target market and agency features (sharing, white label, API).
  3. Order: tools are listed alphabetically, not by score. We don't award ratings, because perceived quality depends heavily on the source photos and the use case.
  4. Conflict of interest: staged.ai is our product, so we spell out its limits and the cases where a competitor is the better fit.
  5. No commercial agreements with any of the other tools listed.

How do you choose the right virtual staging software?

Start with three questions: what do you need to produce, for whom, and how often?

  1. Do you only need staged photos? A focused tool with per-photo pricing is enough.
  2. Do you need video, tours or floor plans? Look at full suites like Pedra or Collov's higher tiers.
  3. Do you need to discuss works and costs with sellers or buyers? You need a tool that combines image and estimate, such as staged.ai for Italian property. See how a virtual renovation works.
  4. Do you resell staging to clients? Check the branded sharing options; we cover this in white label virtual staging.
  5. What's your volume? Compare cost per property, not just the monthly fee.

For context, one industry benchmark puts subscription AI staging at $0.25–3 per photo, against $16–39 per image for staging done by hand by a designer and $2,000–6,000+ a month for physical staging (>aitoolsbakery, 2026). Our guide to how much virtual staging costs has the full breakdown.

Should you hire a virtual staging service or do it yourself?

Doing it yourself with AI software is usually the better choice for everyday listings: one industry benchmark puts subscription AI staging at $0.25–3 per photo, against $16–39 per image for staging done by hand by a designer (aitoolsbakery, 2026). A service makes more sense for a few high-value photos where you want a designer to fine-tune every detail.

  • DIY with AI software: results in minutes, low cost per photo, you choose the style and can regenerate; you are responsible for checking fidelity and labelling the images.
  • Hiring a service: a designer handles furniture choice and retouching, at a higher price per image and with longer turnaround.
  • A middle way: tools like Apply Design combine AI staging with a drag-and-drop editor, so you keep manual control without paying a designer.

Whichever route you choose, the same disclosure rules apply: see our guide to virtual staging disclosure rules, and the full breakdown of how much virtual staging costs.

What should you test during a free trial?

Run the same three to five real photos through every tool you're considering, then check:

  • fidelity: windows, doors, floors, kitchen units and bathroom fittings should match the original;
  • proportions: the room shouldn't look bigger than it is;
  • style consistency across rooms in the same property;
  • export formats that suit property portals and social media;
  • sharing with clients and whether you can use your own branding;
  • transparency: how easy it is to label images as AI-edited. In the UK, estate agents must avoid misleading images under consumer protection law (>Intellopro), and EU rules tighten from 2 August 2026. See our guide to virtual staging disclosure rules.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best virtual staging software?

It depends on your workflow. For an all-in-one suite with video and tours, look at Pedra or Collov AI; for simple photo staging in the US market, Virtual Staging AI; for 3D interior design, Homestyler; for faithful staging with itemised renovation costs on Italian properties, staged.ai. Start from what you need to produce, then compare pricing and free trials.

Is there free virtual staging software?

There are free trials and limited free plans. staged.ai has a free trial with 10 credits and all its main features, REimagineHome offers a few free designs, and Homestyler has a free plan for 3D design. For regular professional use, you'll almost always need a subscription or to pay per image.

How much does virtual staging software cost?

As of October 2026, AI staging subscriptions start at around $16 or €19 a month, while the most complete plans go beyond $79 a month. One industry benchmark puts subscription AI staging at $0.25–3 per photo, compared with $16–39 per image for virtual staging done by hand by a designer.

Which virtual staging software works best outside the US?

Staging an image works anywhere, so most tools are usable internationally. The differences are in currency, language, support and extras. Pedra publishes content in several European languages; staged.ai offers English and Italian, euro pricing and cost estimates calibrated on Italian cities. Several other tools are designed mainly for the US market.

Do I need to disclose that photos were virtually staged?

Yes, with any software. In the EU, Article 50 of the AI Act on AI-generated or manipulated content applies from 2 August 2026; in the UK misleading property marketing is banned; in the US the NAR Code of Ethics and most MLS rules expect disclosure. Label staged images clearly and show the original photo.
