White label virtual staging: sell AI staging under your own brand

White label virtual staging for real estate photographers and agencies: deliver AI staging, cost analysis and quotes under your own logo and brand colours.

Updated 4 October 2026 · staged.ai editorial team · 5 min read

Quick answer

White label virtual staging lets you offer AI-staged photos, room-by-room analysis and quotes to your clients under your own logo and colours, without building any software. With staged.ai you share projects through a branded, read-only link. Pro costs €19 a month; the Agency plan is tailored and adds multi-user access and onboarding.

If you shoot property or holiday-rental photos, you've probably heard this more and more: "Can you show me what it would look like furnished?" White label virtual staging lets you say yes without building software or sending your client to someone else. staged.ai (home-staged.ai) is a web-based AI virtual staging platform built for the Italian market and usable anywhere: you upload the photos, decide what to do with each room and hand your client images, analysis and a quote under your own logo and colours.

Who is white label virtual staging for?

Anyone who already sells a visual service around property and wants to add value without changing trade. Typically:

You are What you can offer under your brand
Real estate photographer A "photos + virtual staging" package for agencies and private sellers
Airbnb / holiday-let photographer Listing retouching plus a restyle proposal with costs and ROI
Estate agency Staging and quotes for your own listings, shared with your branding
Home stager / interior designer A preview of the scheme before physical staging
Property manager Restyle proposals for owners, with versions side by side

The demand is real. In the National Association of REALTORS® >2025 Profile of Home Staging, 83% of buyers' agents said staging helps buyers picture the home as their own, and the most commonly staged rooms were the living room (91%), primary bedroom (83%), dining room (69%) and kitchen (68%). Those are the rooms you shoot on every job.

What do you deliver to your client?

A complete package, not just a picture. For each property you upload up to 10 photos and choose a mode for each one:

  1. Home Staging adds furniture and decor in the style you pick.
  2. Renovation simulates floors, walls and finishes, with full works costs.
  3. Retouch & Declutter improves the photo and removes light clutter, with no quote.
  4. Empty Room removes the furniture, optionally including built-ins such as fitted kitchens and wardrobes, and estimates the clearance cost.

You set budget, style, city and a palette of real Sikkens paint colours. Your client gets:

  • the generated "after" images, downloadable in 4:3, 16:9, 1:1 and 9:16 at 1920 px on the long side (HQ option at 2400 px);
  • a room-by-room analysis with works and costs estimated for the city;
  • a shareable quote and a report with before/after and an estimated total;
  • a shared link with your logo and colours, viewable read-only.

Why does geometric accuracy matter when your name is on it?

Because if an image distorts the room, that's your reputation on the line. staged.ai applies geometric lock: walls, doors, windows, floors, kitchens and fixed bathroom fittings stay put, with dedicated rules for kitchens and bathrooms. The staged photo stays faithful to the real property, which is exactly what your agent or host client needs to be able to show without awkward questions at the viewing.

It also makes disclosure easier. In the EU, AI Act transparency obligations for AI-generated or manipulated images apply from 2 August 2026; in the UK, misleading representations are banned under consumer protection rules (>Intellopro); in the US, the NAR Code of Ethics and local MLS rules generally expect virtual staging to be disclosed. Building a "Virtually staged / AI-edited image" label into your delivery workflow is simply good practice. Our guide to virtual staging disclosure rules covers the detail.

A simple way to package it:

  1. Essential: photo shoot plus Retouch & Declutter on every image.
  2. Plus: Essential plus Home Staging on 2–3 key rooms, with one style variant per room.
  3. Project: Plus with Renovation or Empty Room, a cost analysis and a branded report.

Room variants don't use credits, so you can show the same bedroom in two styles and let the client choose without eating into your margin. Project versions (v1, v2…) let you compare different proposals for the same property.

What does the workflow with a client look like?

A simple one that plugs into the shoot you already do:

  1. Shoot as usual, with natural light and clean framing: good staging starts from a good photo. Our guide to real estate listing photos has a refresher.
  2. Upload the photos and assign each room the mode agreed with your client.
  3. Review the results and pick the best variants. This is where your professional eye earns its fee.
  4. Send the branded link, read-only, so the client sees before and after, the analysis and the quote without being able to change anything.
  5. Deliver the files in the formats the listing site or social channel needs, with the AI label already applied.

If the client changes their mind, create a new project version instead of starting over.

Frequently asked questions

What does white label virtual staging mean?

It means using a virtual staging platform behind the scenes and presenting the results to your client under your brand. In staged.ai, the shared link and what your client sees carry your logo and brand colours, and can be opened read-only. The client deals with you, you stay their single point of contact, and you decide how to package and price the service.

Which staged.ai plan includes white label?

The Pro plan, at €19 a month, includes white-label sharing with your logo and colours, plus monthly credits, unlimited variants and reports. The Agency plan is priced on request and adds full white label, multiple users on one account and onboarding. You can start with the free trial, which gives you 10 credits across all four modes.

How much should I charge clients for virtual staging?

That's your call. As reference points, 2026 industry benchmarks put designer-made virtual staging at $16–39 per image and subscription AI staging at roughly $0.25–3 per photo, while in Italy traditional 3D-studio virtual staging starts from around €200. Factor in your time for selecting and checking images and the value of the cost analysis you deliver.

Do I have to disclose that images are virtually staged?

Yes, as a rule of thumb, and your clients will thank you for it. In the EU, AI Act transparency obligations for AI-manipulated images apply from 2 August 2026. In the UK, misleading property representations are banned under consumer protection law, and in the US the NAR Code of Ethics and many MLSs expect virtual staging to be disclosed. Label every staged image and keep the original.

Does it work for properties outside Italy?

The visual side does: staging, retouching, simulated renovation and emptying a room work on any interior photo. The cost estimates are calibrated on the Italian market, using sources such as OMI, Idealista, FIAIP and EAHSP, and shown in euros. Outside Italy, treat them as a ballpark only and rely on local contractor quotes for real numbers.
