About

Built in Italy, for the Italian market

Not another generic staging app translated from English. staged.ai speaks our market's language: real per-city costs, the supplier palettes you actually use, our own real-estate vocabulary.

The story

From side project to product

staged.ai was born from a concrete problem felt in the field: making listings perform better without paying for physical home staging every time.

  • The start

    A side project

    An internal tool to regenerate a listing's photos and show a property's potential before a sale or rental.

  • The beta

    Opened to the first professionals

    Agents, property managers and home stagers start using it on their real properties. The feedback shapes the product: multi-purpose, localised quotes, branded shareable links.

  • Today

    A real product

    Visual analysis with works and real per-city costs, AI-generated staged photos, versionable projects shareable with your agency's brand. White-label, multi-tenant, built in-house.
