Cookie Policy

Which cookies and tracking tools we use, why, for how long, and how you can control them.

Last updated: 2 June 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that sites store on your device. Together with similar technologies (e.g. localStorage) they make the site work and, where authorised, collect usage statistics. We distinguish between technical cookies (necessary) and analytics cookies (optional, subject to your consent).

2. How we manage consent

On your first visit we show a banner with three options: Accept all, Technical only and Customise. Analytics tools (Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity) are not loaded until you give consent: only after your choice are the relevant scripts activated. Your preference is stored locally in the browser (in localStorage, key hs_cookie_consent) and is not shared with third parties.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time: Manage cookie preferences.

3. Technical cookies (always on)

Necessary for the site and application to function; no consent required (Art. 122 Italian Privacy Code).

6. Managing from the browser

Besides our panel, you can block or delete cookies from your browser settings. Disabling technical cookies may impair the site's functioning.
